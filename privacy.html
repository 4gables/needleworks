Privacy and terms

What is sent, and what is kept

  • Your picture. To turn a picture into stitches, the page sends a copy of it (shrunk to the size it is sewn at) to our server. The server makes the stitches and answers; it does not keep the picture.
  • A design file you open. It is read in your browser. If you ask for a fix, a new size or a re-stitch, its stitches are sent to our server for that, and not kept.
  • The stitch file we make. It is kept for 30 days so you can download it, then deleted.
  • Your download code. We record which designs were downloaded with it, so a design you have already downloaded is free to fetch again. Your browser remembers the code so you don't retype it.
  • Your address on the internet (IP). Counted for a short while to stop one visitor overloading the service, then dropped.

We don't use advertising or tracking, and we don't sell or share anything you send. The page's text fonts come from Google Fonts, which sees your address when your browser fetches them.

Your pictures and your files

Your pictures stay yours. Only send pictures you have the right to embroider. The stitch files you download are yours to sew, for yourself or to sell what you make with them.

Files are checked before they are handed over, but embroidery depends on fabric, stabilizer, thread and machine: sew a test piece before the real one.
