# Embroidery (Brother PE900, 5x7 hoop)

The person using this is a sewist, not a programmer. Talk plainly, never show code, and don't ask
technical questions.

Everything is done on the website: https://4gables.com/needleworks/ (`open` it for her). It runs in her
browser; nothing to install.

## Making a name bookmark (the common case)
1. Look at any inspiration image she gives you: note the lettering style (script? print? thin? bold?),
   thread colour, and whether the name has long lead-in/tail lines.
2. Open https://4gables.com/needleworks/#bookmark (or **Design your own**, then the **Bookmark** card). Tell her to
   tap the name in **Pieces** and type it in **Words** exactly as she wants it stitched, then pick the closest
   **Font**. Sacramento matches thin monoline scripts; fonts marked "like …" resemble popular embroiderydesigns.com
   fonts. The `~` at each end of the name is a swash (the long line before or after it): **Swash before** /
   **Swash after** under Words take them off or put them back. Quicksand and Josefin Sans are print (non-cursive)
   fonts; their lines are straight dashes. For a blank bookmark, remove the name piece (✕). Full size needs the
   5x7 hoop.
3. She picks the thread colour (Thread, for the bookmark piece and the name), checks the preview (it can **Watch it sew**), and presses **Download**.
4. Tell her to copy the downloaded `.pes` file onto a USB stick, not inside a folder.
5. Give her the stitching steps below, with the name filled in (the site also shows them under
   "How to stitch it").

## Other designs
- **Picture to stitches**: logos, clip art and simple drawings with a few solid colours (not photos).
  **Rotate picture** turns it a quarter turn; **Turn hoop** lays the hoop sideways so a wide design can use the
  long side (the file still loads the right way on the machine). The 4x4 hoop can be picked at the top.
- **Open a design**: preview any PES/PEC/DST she already has, check it fits the 5x7 hoop, or convert DST to PES.

## Stitching steps to give her (bookmark)
Supplies: tear-away stabilizer, front fabric ~3x8 in, felt for the back ~3x8 in, thread plus a
matching bobbin, tape, small sharp scissors, ~12 in of 3/8 in ribbon.
Hoop only the stabilizer. On the PE900: USB, pick the file, Set (don't rotate/resize). Keep the same
thread in the whole time. The 3 "colors" on screen are pauses:
1. It sews the outline on the stabilizer.
2. Tape the front fabric on, right side up, press Start. It tacks the fabric down and sews the name. Remove the
   hoop (leave everything hooped), trim close to the line, put the hoop back.
3. Take the hoop off and turn it over. Tape the felt over the back, covering the outline by 1/2 in. Turn it back,
   put it on, press Start: it tacks the felt and sews the ribbon hole and the satin border through every layer.
Finish: tear away stabilizer, cut round the bookmark close to the satin, snip fuzz, dab Fray Check inside the ring,
let it dry, cut out the ribbon hole, loop the ribbon through. (The site's "How to stitch it" says the same.)
