Class guarded: pictures unlike the 8 Etsy samples break the Picture tab (round 8: a logo's apostrophe and 1 px rule lost, crayon strokes traced on both edges, garbled 120 mm letters failing the checker; etsy_4/6/7's colours). This page draws a seeded corpus of our own pictures (logos in three weights and 1-3 colours, apostrophes and dots, thin rules, crayon and marker strokes, outlines and fills, gradients, soft shadows, photo noise, JPEG artefacts, transparent PNGs, 50 px and 6000 px images, one colour, two near-identical colours, a coloured frame, pastel lines, dark pictures), knowing where every colour region and text piece is, and sends each one through the Picture tab's own path (contentBox, the learn copy, picturePlan, the refit, and js/worker.js) in all three styles at a few sizes and colour counts. Per case it checks: the file passes quality.js and validatePES (and is POSTed for tests/pe900_check.py); every colour region big enough to sew is sewn, or the app says why (a line finer than thread); text pieces survive; crayon strokes are sewn along their centre in a line drawing; backdrops and frames aren't sewn; each region's thread is in its drawn colour's hue family, and no thread is one nobody drew; trims stay within a budget per piece and per cm²; it finishes in time.
