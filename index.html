    One word works best. Type it the way you want it stitched: all lowercase flows most gracefully in script.

    How to stitch it · 5 steps, 4 pauses

    Keep the same thread in the whole time. The machine pauses 4 times. Each pause is a fabric step.

    Have ready: tear-away stabilizer, cotton for the front and felt for the back (each about 3 × 8 in), tape, Fray Check and a ribbon.

      Finish: tear away the stabilizer and snip any fuzz. Dab Fray Check inside the ribbon ring, let it dry, then cut out the hole and loop a ribbon through.

        Start from

        First time with appliqué?
        • Have ready: stabilizer (tear-away; cut-away for stretchy fabric), a piece of cotton or felt 1 in bigger than the shape, and small curved scissors.
        • Hoop the base fabric with the stabilizer under it, smooth and tight like a drum.
        • At each pause the machine stops for you. Take the whole hoop off to trim, but never take the fabric out of the hoop.
        • Trim about 1 mm (a bare 1/16 in) outside the stitching, as close as you can without cutting it: further out and it shows past the satin edge.
        • Cotton frays less with iron-on fusible web on the back; felt doesn't fray at all.
        • Check the size on the machine's screen before you press Start.
