# Embroidery (Brother PE900, 5x7 hoop)

The person using this is a sewist, not a programmer. Talk plainly, never show code, and don't ask
technical questions.

Everything is done on the website: https://4gables.com/needleworks/ (`open` it for her). It runs in her
browser; nothing to install.

## Making a name bookmark (the common case)
1. Look at any inspiration image she gives you: note the lettering style (script? print? thin? bold?),
   thread colour, and whether the name has long lead-in/tail lines.
2. Open https://4gables.com/needleworks/#bookmark (or **Design your own**, then the **Name bookmark** card). Tell her to type the name exactly as she wants it stitched and to
   pick the closest font. Sacramento matches thin monoline scripts; fonts marked "like …" resemble
   popular embroiderydesigns.com fonts. If the long line before or after the name looks wrong, untick
   **Swash before the name** or **Swash after the name** (either or both). Quicksand and Josefin Sans are print
   (non-cursive) fonts; their lines are straight dashes. **Blank bookmark, no name** makes one with no lettering
   (3 pauses instead of 4). The bookmark needs the 5x7 hoop.
3. She picks the thread colour, checks the preview (it can **Watch it sew**), and presses **Download**.
4. Tell her to copy the downloaded `.pes` file onto a USB stick, not inside a folder.
5. Give her the stitching steps below, with the name filled in (the site also shows them under
   "How to stitch it").

## Other designs
- **Picture to stitches**: logos, clip art and simple drawings with a few solid colours (not photos).
  **Rotate picture** turns it a quarter turn; **Turn hoop** lays the hoop sideways so a wide design can use the
  long side (the file still loads the right way on the machine). The 4x4 hoop can be picked at the top.
- **Open a design**: preview any PES/PEC/DST she already has, check it fits the 5x7 hoop, or convert DST to PES.

## Stitching steps to give her (bookmark)
Supplies: tear-away stabilizer, front fabric ~3x8 in, felt for the back ~3x8 in, thread plus a
matching bobbin, tape, small sharp scissors, ~12 in of 3/8 in ribbon.
Hoop only the stabilizer. On the PE900: USB, pick the file, Set (don't rotate/resize). Keep the same
thread in the whole time. The 5 "colors" on screen are pauses:
1. It sews the outline on the stabilizer.
2. Tape the front fabric on, right side up, press Start. Remove the hoop (leave everything hooped),
   trim ~1/8 in outside the line, put the hoop back.
3. It sews the name.
4. Tape felt to the back of the hoop, press Start. Remove the hoop, trim the felt ~1/8 in outside the line, put it back.
5. It sews the ribbon hole and border.
Finish: tear away stabilizer, snip fuzz, cut out the ribbon hole, loop the ribbon through.
